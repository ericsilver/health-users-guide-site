Corrections

The book isn't published yet, so there is nothing to correct here. When it is, every correction will be listed on this page with the date, the page, what it said, and what it should have said. Rules in this subject change every year; the book dates each one "as of this edition," and this page is where the edition gets updated between printings.

To report one, use the contact form. Corrections are the most useful thing you can send.
