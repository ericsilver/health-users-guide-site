Write to me

Anything sent here reaches me. You don’t have to say who you are. If you’d like a reply, leave a way to reach you.

Corrections are the most useful thing you can send. Please don’t send the details of your own medical situation. I’m not a clinician or a lawyer, I can’t advise you, and this form isn’t confidential the way a doctor’s office is. If it’s an emergency, call 911; if it’s a crisis, call or text 988.

Messages travel through a form relay to my inbox. The relay sees the text you type and your connection details. I see only the text.
