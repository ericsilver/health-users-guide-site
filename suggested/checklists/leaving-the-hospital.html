Before you leave the hospital

A checklist to print

Leave with your prescriptions, a care plan, and the name and number of someone to call.

  1. Make four lists from the discharge summary: medicines to take, with dose and timing; medicines to stop; follow-up visits and tests, with who sets up each one; and the warning signs that mean coming back. prompt-discharge.txt
  2. If anything on those lists is unclear or contradicts something else you were told, ask before you go.
  3. Medicare inpatient, and sure it’s too soon? Call the quality-improvement organization listed on your Important Message from Medicare, on the day of discharge at the latest. A phone request counts. Then confirm it in writing. discharge-appeal.txt
  4. If you ask for that review in time, you owe nothing for the extra days while it’s pending, apart from your usual cost sharing.
  5. Once you’re home, message your primary-care doctor through the portal about the stay, and ask whether they need to see you. prompt-primary-care.txt
  6. Ask for your records: the discharge summary now, and the full chart, which the hospital has thirty days to send. records.txt
  7. When the bill for the stay arrives, start with the checklist Before you pay a hospital bill.

General information about how health care works, not medical or legal advice. Nothing here should delay care in an emergency. The letters named here are free at healthusersguide.com.
